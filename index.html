Chapter By Chapter

Our First Year

Every scroll turns another page.

  1. I

    Where it began.

    Your first ever hand made gift to me that was from Chateau Elysse. Sweet, creative, caring, and joyful. One of the personalities of yours that I adore so much. How the world is so clever for showing signs of how meant to be we are together.

  2. II

    Memories that'll never fade.

    It consists of your cute photographs when you were younger, these were times where you had showcased your pretty patootie side. It also consists of our photobooths that are memorable to me, and last but not the least, your outstanding, fabulous, and gorgeous look at your 18th birthday celebration.

  3. III

    Happy Family

    I'm for sure that in the future, we'll have wonderful kids, fluffy cute dogs, a big mansion, great source of incomes, and a peaceful happy life. It's not a "what if" for me anymore, it's a for sure thing that I see that we would accomplish together in the future.

  4. IV

    Shortcomings will always strengthen us.

    We may not be perfect in our own perspectives, but the uniqueness that we hold is what makes us better and worth the wait for each other. The way we present ourselves in everyday struggles, is amusing and I will always expect us to grow stronger and be better.

  5. V

    You're taking me to paradise.

    With every step and moments that we cherish, I always remember the times where I offer you my help and I show that my acts of service will never go away. It's in every small gestures that I do, that shows the patience and love that I have for you.

  6. VI

    I only glow when you're around

    The times where you show me how much you value me and how much I've improved towards the months we've been together, warms my heart in a specific way that I can never distinguish with words. The beauty of it is in the eye of the beholder and I'm honoured to be the beholder of your love.

  7. VII

    I always will show up

    They say that when life strikes you down, you just have to stand back up. That's true for most cases, but if you ever feel tired, you don't have to worry with where you would get the strength back up, because I'm here to support and help you in every single way.

  8. VIII

    You're the brightest star I search for every night.

    In a lot of nights and days that passes by, we can't see each other very often. I feel sad and deprived of your physical presence as those days passes by, but you never cut short, you always made up for the time that we couldn't spend in our physical presence. I love that so much.

  9. IX

    Malayo na, pero malayo pa.

    The reason why the "Present" is called "Present" is because everyday that we have together is a gift. You were the only great thing that has ever happened to me in my entire life and I can never take for granted, the gift that was given to me, and that is you.

  10. X

    Happy Anniversary.

    I said it before and I will say it again: I will marry you soon. These rings showed the depth of our commitment, although we haven't found your ring yet, I'm sure in a specific timeline, we'll find that ring, paired with my engagement ring. I very much hope to see you in a wedding gown in the future, Happy 1st Anniversary.

"I will marry you soon, so hold on tight."

For Cali

Mahal na mahal.
Mahal na ang bigas sabi nga sa isang kanta. Ngunit ako yung tipong taong hindi masasayangan kapag ginastusan ka. Ako yung taong magsisilbing pahinga, tuwing napapagod na ang iyong puso at mga mata. Tuwid ako sa aking sanaysay sa buhay, tayo ay magsasama, mag-mamahalan, at magpapahinga sa isang bahay. Mahal kita, at hindi magbabago ang pagtingin ko sa iyong mga mata. May mga oras man na hindi ko maibigay lahat sa iyo' ngunit susubukan at gagawan ng paraan na maibigay ang ikakasaya ng iyong puso. Unang anibersaryo natin mahal ko, hindi ka manghihinayang sa pinagsamahan natin hanggang dulo. Hawakan ako at huwag bitiwan, ikaw ay aking pakakasalan.

Officially 1 Year with you. Happy Anniversary my baby, live, laugh, and love.

Forever yours, Mikhail.
